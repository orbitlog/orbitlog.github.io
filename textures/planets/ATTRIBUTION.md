# Planet Texture Attribution

Planet and sun surface textures in this folder are from Solar System Scope:

https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/

Solar System Scope states that the textures are distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license.

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Files used:

- `sun.webp`
- `mercury.webp`
- `venus.webp`
- `earth.webp`
- `earth_clouds.webp`
- `mars.webp`
- `jupiter.webp`
- `saturn.webp`
- `saturn_ring.png`
- `uranus.webp`
- `neptune.webp`

Surface maps are WebP derivatives of the original JPEG textures (quality 82, original 2048 × 1024 resolution). Saturn’s ring remains the original PNG.
